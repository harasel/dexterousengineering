About Dexterous Engineering

Engineering Experience. Technical Expertise. Practical Solutions.

A mechanical and industrial engineering company in Bangladesh, providing integrated solutions across heating, cooling, power and water treatment since 2002.

Our Story

Serving Bangladesh’s Industrial Sector Since 2002

Dexterous Engineering has been operating in Bangladesh since 2002 as a mechanical and industrial engineering company. The company provides integrated engineering solutions across four core areas: Heating, Cooling, Power and Water Treatment.

Our focus is on delivering environment-friendly energy solutions that meet the practical engineering requirements of industrial applications.

The company works with established global technology brands and provides end-to-end engineering services including consultancy, equipment supply, installation, commissioning, maintenance and technical support.

Dexterous Engineering’s scope covers boilers, chillers, cooling towers, pumps, heat exchangers, water treatment chemicals, circuit breakers, PFI systems and PLC automation.

Vision & Mission

Our Engineering Philosophy

Our Vision

Core Areas

Four Pillars of Engineering

Dexterous Engineering provides integrated solutions across four interconnected areas of industrial engineering.

Heating

Boilers, thermal systems and heat exchange equipment for industrial process and utility heating applications.

Cooling

Chillers, cooling towers and cooling systems for process cooling and climate control in industrial environments.

Power

Circuit breakers, PFI systems, PLC automation and co-generation solutions for industrial power management.

Water Treatment

Water treatment chemicals, ETP-related solutions and wastewater systems for industrial water management.
